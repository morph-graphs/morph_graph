Is every machine's worker alive?

One row per board pool. A worker writes a heartbeat about every minute; over 30 minutes without one while it holds tasks is flagged. Done counts tasks finished in the last 24 hours (for arm00 and Zaratan, memories that appeared).

Which tasks are where?

Every open task, in the board's priority order. Ready tasks go to the first pool listed for them that has a free slot and runs that baseline; a task that failed twice is held until someone fixes the cause and resets it.
